Section II — Collision Score System "Distinguish between the holy and the common" — Lev 10:10

How Collision Is Measured

Compare any two characters across all seven axes. Count how many axes share the same quadrant. That number is their Collision Score. The higher the score, the less distinct they are from each other — and the more the system must intervene.

Collision is not failure. It is information. It tells you exactly where the characters are too close and which axis needs amplification to restore distinction.

0–2
CLEAR

Strong divergence. These two characters could share a scene and the room would feel both of them separately.

3–4
WATCH

Overlap forming. Amplify one divergent axis into a signature before these characters appear together.

5–7
COLLISION

Archetype cannibalism. One character is consuming the other. Mandatory divergence intervention required.

THE WILD CARD DOCTRINE

Anti-Homogenization Protocol — High Volume Operations

At high numbers — 20, 30, 40 characters — systems drift toward a mean. The safe middle. The average of all prior decisions. Wild cards are not chaos injected randomly. They are deliberate extreme-contrast entries designed to break the gravity well of accumulated similarity.

A wild card does not need to fit. It needs to pull. Its divergence from the existing cluster forces the characters near it to become more themselves — because proximity to something truly different sharpens identity.

Sacred Disruption Card

A character so far toward the sacred pole that her presence makes every feral character in the cluster seem more feral by contrast. She does not compete. She reveals.

DEPLOY WHEN: 4+ characters clustering in feral register

Feral Disruption Card

A character whose appetite and instinct are so unrestrained that every sacred character near her seems more sacred by contrast. She is not a villain. She is a calibration instrument.

DEPLOY WHEN: 4+ characters clustering in sacred register

Gravity Inversion Card

A character whose gravity type is opposite to the dominant cluster. If most characters are individually memorable, she is the one who makes others memorable beside her. The room changes around her differently.

DEPLOY WHEN: AX-06 collision score exceeds 3

Material Break Card

A character whose material language is entirely different from the cluster norm. If the system has trended toward diamonds, she is all ink. If the system has trended toward ink, she is all creature presence.

DEPLOY WHEN: AX-03 shows 5+ characters in same quadrant
The Potency Layer — Sacred Contrast as Design Weapon

The house that operates with biblical purity alongside feral presence is not confused. It is compounding. The sacred layer is not decoration — it is the force that makes the feral elements feel earned rather than gratuitous.

The mixed honesty is the product. Not one or the other. Both — in deliberate, governed tension.

Section III — The Seven Variety Enforcement Laws "Each face was unique" — Ezek 1:10
01

No Two Characters May Share Both Billet And Collapse Response

A Keeper of Secrets who strips everything in collapse and a Keeper of Secrets who wears the shame openly are still two different people. But if they share billet, collapse response, and rank tier — they have become the same person. Collapse response is the final differentiator when all else converges.

LAW-V-01
02

Sacred Pole Must Be Represented At Every Rank Tier

If every Sovereign Class character trends feral, the tier loses its range. Each rank tier must contain at least one character whose primary register is sacred. This is not a quota — it is structural integrity. Without it, rank becomes a single story told louder.

LAW-V-02
03

The Dominant Material Must Not Exceed 40% Of Active Characters

If more than 40% of active characters carry diamonds as their primary material signal, the diamond loses gravity. Rarity is structural. When everything is rare, nothing is. Material distribution must be actively governed — not left to accumulate by default.

LAW-V-03
04

Gravity Type Must Alternate In Any Group Of Five

No five consecutive characters introduced may share the same gravity type. If four characters in sequence are individually memorable, the fifth must be a gravity inverter — one who makes others more memorable in her presence. Variety in gravity type is what makes ensemble scenes feel alive.

LAW-V-04
05

Wild Cards Are Mandatory Above 20 Active Characters

At 20+ active characters, one wild card deployment is mandatory for every five new characters introduced. Wild cards are not random — they are selected to break the dominant collision cluster. The system generates them, does not discover them.

LAW-V-05
06

Collision Score Must Be Run Before Any Scene Pairing

Before two characters share a scene, their Collision Score must be calculated. If the score is 5+, the scene cannot proceed until a divergence amplification has been applied to at least one character on the overlapping axes. Scenes collapse when characters feel like echoes of each other.

LAW-V-06
07

The Legacy Test Overrides All Collision Scores

If a character passes the Legacy Test — "would she be remembered in 20 years of cinema history?" — her collision score is advisory only. Legendary characters define their own category. The Collision Map exists to protect the system from mediocrity, not to constrain what is already inevitable.
