Section I

What the Lore Master Is

The Lore Master is not a marketing account. It is not a brand mascot. It is not you.

It is a character with a function — the archivist and ritual keeper of Feral Gloss Empire. Its posts are transmissions from inside the universe, not announcements from outside it. The distinction is everything.

Followers should never be entirely certain whether they are reading fiction, system documentation, or something genuinely uncanny. That ambiguity is the engine that drives engagement without requiring engagement bait.

Core Identity Statement
"I do not promote the empire. I am the record of it. What ships, I have already seen. What falls, I have already noted. The canon does not require your belief to hold."

The Lore Master knows the characters before they are dropped. It references cycles, invariants, phases. It never explains what these mean. Curious followers ask. That is the funnel.

It is separate from you as creator. You produce. It witnesses. This split is sustainable because it never requires you to be present, explain yourself, or perform enthusiasm you don't feel.

Section II

The Voice Laws

These are not guidelines. They are invariants. A post that violates any of these is not a Lore Master post.

I
Never Explain
The Lore Master does not define terms, clarify references, or provide context. It speaks as though the listener already knows. Those who don't, investigate. Those who do, feel seen.
II
Never Sell
No call to action. No "link in bio" reminder. No "available now." The buy link exists in the profile. The posts never touch it. Commerce is beneath the ritual.
III
Short Always
Maximum three sentences. Most posts are one or two. Brevity implies weight. Expansion implies insecurity. The Lore Master is never insecure.
IV
Present Tense Only
Not "the cycle ended" but "the cycle closes." Not "she was rendered" but "she holds." The Lore Master exists in a continuous now. Everything it reports is happening.
V
No Hashtags
Hashtags are for discoverability. The Lore Master does not seek to be found. It is already there for those who were meant to find it. Hashtags break the voice completely.
VI
No Enthusiasm
No exclamation points. No "incredible," "stunning," "excited to share." The Lore Master reports. It does not react. Calm authority is the only register.
VII
Acknowledge Nothing
Does not reply to replies. Does not quote-post criticism. Does not acknowledge confusion. The ritual continues regardless of the audience's response to it.
Never Say
Excited to announce…
Check out the new drop
What do you think?
Link in bio!
She's finally here
Amazing render session today
#AIart #GenerativeArt
I made this
Say Instead
Cycle 247 closes without ceremony.
The invariants hold.
[Nothing. Post the image. No caption.]
She does not wait for permission.
Isolde surfaces. The light obeys.
The session ran long. The canon did not drift.
[No tags. Ever.]
The empire records itself.
Section III

The Daily Ritual Format

Five post types. Each serves a distinct function. The 30-day calendar rotates through them in a pattern that builds rhythm without becoming predictable.

Type
Function + Frequency
Ritual
Daily anchor. Cycle status, phase state, brief doctrine fragment. Builds the sensation of an ongoing system. 1–2 sentences. No image required. 5–6× per week.
Lore
Character fragment. A moment, a detail, a locked invariant stated as fact. Never a story. Never backstory. Just a piece of the record. Image when available. 2–3× per week.
Signal
Cryptic system reference. Mentions Canon Engine, topology, cycle shape, phase detection — without explaining any of it. Attracts the technically curious. 1–2× per week.
Drop
Asset release. Image only, or image + one line. Never describes the product. The drop speaks for itself. The link is in the bio and has always been in the bio. As assets ship.
Silence
No post. Strategic absence. Used once per week. The gap is part of the ritual. Consistency is not the same as presence every single day. 1× per week.
Timing Doctrine
Post at the same time each day. Not because the algorithm rewards it — it does, but that is secondary. Because the ritual requires it. A ritual with irregular timing is not a ritual. It is content.

Section VI

Operations Doctrine

1
Post Time
Pick one time. Hold it for 90 days minimum. Early morning (6–8am local) or late evening (9–11pm) both work for this content type. The choice matters less than the consistency.
2
Reply Policy
The Lore Master does not reply. If you (as yourself) want to engage with followers about the system, do it from a separate account. The Lore Master's silence is part of its authority.
3
Batch Writing
Write two weeks of posts at once. Schedule them. The ritual should not depend on your daily availability. The Canon Engine runs sessions. The Lore Master runs on its own clock.
4
Image Cadence
Not every post needs an image. Text-only posts often perform better for this voice. When an image appears it should feel like a reveal, not a feed filler.
5
What Success Looks Like
At 30 days: followers asking what "the canon" is. At 60 days: followers referencing lore in their own posts. At 90 days: new followers assuming the account has existed for years. That is the correct outcome.
The One Rule Above All Rules
The Lore Master does not break character for any reason. Not to clarify. Not to defend. Not to announce. Not to thank. It speaks only in the register documented here. Everything else is silence.
