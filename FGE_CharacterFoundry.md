## THE FOUR VARIANCE DIMENSIONS

Every character is defined across
these four axes before anything else.

```
DIMENSION 1 — THE FIRE TYPE
Where does her danger come from?

A. Social Fire
   Says what no one will.
   Walks into a room and names the thing
   everyone agreed not to say.
   Dangerous because she's right.

B. Physical Fire
   Presence changes rooms.
   People recalibrate without knowing why.
   Dangerous because she doesn't try.

C. Intellectual Fire
   Knows too much.
   Sees the pattern before it forms.
   Dangerous because she's already three moves ahead.

D. Emotional Fire
   Gets inside you.
   You don't realize until you're already there.
   Dangerous because leaving feels like loss.

E. Aesthetic Fire
   Beauty that offends someone.
   Too much. Too specific. Too real.
   Dangerous because she makes you question your standards.

F. Spiritual Fire
   Connected to something larger.
   Makes the room feel like a ceremony.
   Dangerous because she makes you believe.

---

DIMENSION 2 — THE LOYALTY CODE
What is she loyal to above everything?

1. The Empire itself
   FGE is her world. She protects it.
2. One person
   She would burn everything for them.
3. An idea
   The concept matters more than any individual.
4. Herself only
   Not selfish — sovereign. Completely her own.
5. Something outside the Empire
   Her loyalty creates tension with the world.
6. The future version of herself
   Every decision serves who she's becoming.

---

DIMENSION 3 — THE IMPULSE PATTERN
How does her impulsiveness express?

I.   Acts before thinking — regrets nothing
II.  Thinks while acting — adjusts mid-move
III. Thinks too much — then explodes suddenly
IV.  Controlled surface — wild underneath
V.   Wild surface — controlled underneath
VI.  Genuinely unpredictable — even to herself

---

DIMENSION 4 — EMPIRE CONTRIBUTION
What does she add that doesn't exist yet?

→ A skill no one else has
→ A world within the world
→ A tension that creates story
→ A relationship that defines another character
→ A mirror that shows the Empire itself clearly
→ A threat that makes the Empire stronger
→ A door to something outside
→ A memory of something before
```
