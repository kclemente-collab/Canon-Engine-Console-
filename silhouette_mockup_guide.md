## CANVA MOBILE CREATION WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Setup (5 minutes)
1. **Download Canva** (free iOS app)
2. **Create new design** → Custom size: 1080x1080 (square)
3. **Search "silhouette"** in elements
4. **Find human figure silhouette** (side view, standing)
5. **Make it black** (#000000)
6. **Save as "Frame 1"**

### Step 2: Create Base Human (Frame 1)
**Silhouette Specifications**:
- **Height**: 70% of canvas height
- **Position**: Centered, facing right
- **Color**: Solid black (#000000)
- **Posture**: Upright, natural standing
- **Clean profile**: No unnecessary details

**Add Technical Annotations**:
- **Timeline**: "0 seconds - Human Base"
- **Systems Active**: "N+5 Quantum Field - Standby"
- **Energy Level**: "Normal - 100 units"

## CANVA MOBILE TIPS

### Efficient Workflow:
1. **Always duplicate** previous frame before editing
2. **Use two fingers** to rotate and resize precisely
3. **Zoom in** for detailed shape adjustments
4. **Save frequently** to avoid losing work
5. **Work in good lighting** for screen visibility

### Shape Modification Techniques:
1. **Stretch horizontally** for head elongation
2. **Rotate entire figure** for posture changes
3. **Use corner handles** for proportional scaling
4. **Pinch and stretch** individual body parts
5. **Add simple shapes** for tail, ears

### Professional Finishing:
1. **Consistent black color** across all frames
2. **Clean, readable annotations**
3. **Proper spacing and alignment**
4. **Professional font** (Helvetica/Arial)
5. **Grid layout** for final presentation
