ASCEN
SION

Characters do not begin legendary. They become it. This module governs how they earn rank, accumulate gravity, and survive — or define — their own collapse.

Beauty is assigned. History is earned.

A character at Rank I and a character at Rank VI may wear the same face. But the room does not treat them the same. The difference lives in their ink, their animals, their stones — and in the silence that follows them when they enter.

This law governs that silence.

I
Section I — The Material Trinity

Rank in FGE is not declared. It is visible. Three materials carry the record of a character's advancement. They cannot be faked. They cannot be borrowed. They can only be earned — or lost through collapse.

INK
Permanent Identity

Tattoos chosen at each rank threshold. Irreversible. The body becomes a ledger of what she has survived, decided, and become. Ink is the most honest material — it cannot be removed without leaving a scar of the removal.

RULE 01 — ink is chosen, never assigned
RULE 02 — each mark has a specific meaning
RULE 03 — marks accumulate visibly per rank
RULE 04 — collapse-ink inverts in meaning, not removal
PET
Living Proof

Rare animals that require resources, trust, and knowledge to keep alive. The presence of a rare pet signals access — to networks, to funding, to expertise others cannot reach. They are not accessories. They are living proof of station.

RULE 01 — pets are rank-gated, not purchasable
RULE 02 — the rarer the animal, the higher required rank
RULE 03 — if the pet dies, rank signal weakens
RULE 04 — collapse may require the pet's return
◆
Compressed History

Diamonds are portable sovereignty. Each stone has a provenance — where it came from, what event it marks, who gave it or what it was traded for. A character's diamonds are a silent autobiography worn on the body.

RULE 01 — diamonds have named provenance
RULE 02 — gifts carry the giver's gravity
RULE 03 — traded diamonds carry transaction memory
RULE 04 — collapse diamonds become shame trophies or proof
RANK IS NOT WORN. IT IS READ.
II
Section II — The Six Ranks of Ascension

Six ranks. Each one a different relationship to power. The room responds differently to each. Advancement is not automatic — it requires a Trigger Event and the accumulation of all prior material marks.

INITIATE
Rank I — She has arrived
Material: First ink mark (chosen, small, personal). No pet. No diamonds yet.
Gravity: None assigned — she is still learning what the room expects.
Room reads her as: New. Interesting. Unproven.
R — I
ATTENDANT
Rank II — She has survived something
Material: Second ink (larger, visible in public). First small rare animal.
Gravity: Quiet presence — others notice she does not perform.
Room reads her as: Capable. Worth watching.
R — II
TRUSTED HAND
Rank III — She knows something you don't
Material: Third ink (signature placement). First diamond (provenance known). Rare pet upgraded.
Gravity: Others lower their voices when she enters. Not fear. Respect with edges.
Room reads her as: Dangerous to disappoint.
R — III
INNER CIRCLE
Rank IV — She has been chosen and chose back
Material: Ink becomes a system (coherent visual language). Diamond collection (2–3 stones, each named). Rare pet with known lineage.
Gravity: She changes the energy of a room by entering it. Visual field shifts.
Room reads her as: Someone with leverage she hasn't used yet.
R — IV
APEX PRESENCE
Rank V — She does not need the room's approval
Material: Ink tells a complete story (readable as narrative). Signature diamond (one stone above all others). Rare pet that others recognize on sight.
Gravity: Conversation shifts when she speaks. Silence when she stops.
Room reads her as: The standard everyone else is measured against.
R — V
SOVEREIGN CLASS
Rank VI — The room was built for her
Material: Full ink biography (body as complete record). Diamond legacy (collection with historical weight). Rare pet that is itself a legend in the Empire.
Gravity: Others dress differently knowing she will be present. The aesthetic field of the room reorganizes around her.
Room reads her as: Inevitable.
R — VI ◆
III
Section III — Visual Gravity Architecture

Gravity in FGE is visual. The room does not feel her — it sees her differently. This section defines exactly what changes, aesthetically, as rank increases.

SILHOUETTE WEIGHT

Higher rank characters occupy more visual space — not physically larger, but their clothing, their ink, their animal create a silhouette that commands peripheral attention. The eye finds them first.

R1–R2 → simple lines, body as canvas
R3–R4 → layered presence, ink visible
R5–R6 → silhouette is immediately iconic
MATERIAL DENSITY

Diamonds catch light differently in high-rank characters — not because of the stones themselves, but because of what they represent. Provenance makes light feel heavier. The room registers this without knowing why.

R1–R2 → no diamonds or unprovenanced
R3–R4 → one or two named stones
R5–R6 → collection with historical weight
INK NARRATIVE

At low ranks, ink is decoration. At high ranks, ink is biography. Others who know the Empire can read rank directly from placement, scale, and accumulation of marks. Ink transforms the body into a public record.

R1 → one mark, personal meaning
R3 → marks visible, placement intentional
R6 → full narrative, body as archive
CREATURE PRESENCE

A rare pet at Rank V is not a pet. It is a credential. Others recognize the animal before they place the face. The creature signals network access, financial reach, and knowledge depth that no diamond or ink can replicate.

R2 → small rare animal, personal bond
R4 → rare animal with known lineage
R6 → creature is itself a legend
IV
Section IV — Ascension Trigger Events

Rank does not advance by time served. It advances through Trigger Events — specific acts that cannot be faked and cannot be repeated. Each trigger unlocks one rank threshold.

◈

The Act of Restraint

She had the power to destroy someone or something — and chose not to. Not from weakness. From decision. Others witnessed the restraint and understood the power behind it. This is the most common first trigger.

◈

The Public Test

She was tested in a room full of people who wanted her to fail. She did not fail. She did not perform. She simply was — and the room recalibrated. No announcement. Just the aftermath.

◈

The Cost Paid

She gave something up that others assumed she would protect at all costs. The sacrifice was real. The loss was visible. What she gained in gravity was worth more than what she surrendered.

◈

The Knowledge Held

She knows something that, if spoken, would change everything for everyone in the room. She has never spoken it. Others sense she holds something. They do not know what. The not-knowing is the source of her gravity.

◈

The Room Remembered

A room she left is still talking about her. Not about what she did — about what she was. This trigger is discovered, not achieved. One day the evidence arrives: she has become a reference point for others.

V
Section V — The Law of Collapse

Betrayal & Collapse

Rank regression occurs only through Betrayal or Collapse. These are not mistakes. They are structural failures — the kind that change what others believe is possible from her.

When collapse occurs, the character faces the single most revealing decision in her arc:

What does she keep?

The choice of what to retain, return, or display inverted is not a personal preference. It is a rank signal visible to everyone who knows the Empire's language.

STRIP EVERYTHING

She returns the diamonds. She covers the ink. She releases the animal. She starts again from nothing — but from a position of dignity.

SIGNAL: HONOR OVER HISTORY

KEEP THE WOUND

She keeps one mark — the one that cost her everything. Wears it openly. Inverted or not, it is hers. She has decided that her collapse is part of her record, not a deletion of it.

SIGNAL: TRUTH OVER COMFORT

WEAR THE SHAME

She keeps everything. Lets others read the inverted meaning. Refuses to hide what happened. The marks now mean something different — and she knows it, and walks anyway.

SIGNAL: POWER OVER APPROVAL
VI
Section VI — Billet Assignment

Rank is vertical. Billets are horizontal. Two characters at the same rank can have entirely different functional power based on their billet. Billets prevent archetype collision — they are the reason no two Sovereign Class characters feel the same.

Keeper of Secrets

She holds information that changes the gravity of everyone near her. Her power is in what she has never said.

Diplomatic Blade

She moves between rooms that would otherwise never communicate. Her presence is the bridge — and the threat that keeps the bridge intact.

Emotional Anchor

Others orient toward her in crisis. Not because she solves problems — because her stillness makes panic feel temporary.

Memory Holder

She remembers everything. What was said, what was meant, what was promised and broken. Her memory is her leverage.

Social Predator

She reads a room faster than anyone. She knows who needs what from whom. She is three moves ahead before the introduction ends.

Succession Guardian

She decides who rises. Not loudly. Through patience, placement, and the strategic withholding of her endorsement until the right moment.

"If this character walked into cinema history, would people remember her in 20 years?"

If no — rebuild. That is the only benchmark that matters.
"Beauty attracts attention. History creates obsession. Build for obsession."
