Pre-verbal Signal Architecture

VIBE
KERNEL

The irreducible frequency a character emits before the brain engages. Not identity. Not archetype. The one thing felt before anything is known. It survives rank, collapse, billet change, and time. It cannot be designed away.

Identity can drift. Archetype can be reassigned. Rank can collapse. The Vibe Kernel cannot change.

It is not what she is. It is what the room feels the moment she enters — before she has spoken, moved, or been introduced. One sentence. Ten words maximum. The frequency beneath everything else.

The Five Kernel Laws
01
One sentence. Ten words maximum. If it takes more than ten words, it is not a Vibe Kernel — it is a character description. Kernels are compression, not summary.
02
It must be felt, not understood. A Vibe Kernel that requires explanation has already failed. The right sentence lands before the mind processes it.
03
It must survive collapse. If the character loses everything — rank, material, billet — the Kernel remains true. It is what she was before she earned anything and what she will be after she loses it.
04
No two Kernels may use the same structural tension. If two characters both carry the form "X that the room suspects is Y" — one must be rewritten. Kernel collision is the deepest form of archetype cannibalism.
05
The Kernel is written last, not first. You discover it after the character exists — not before. It is extracted from what is already true, never imposed from outside.
Anatomy Of A Kernel — Dissection

THE SURFACE SIGNAL
"Warmth"

What the room receives first. Disarming. Safe register. The brain relaxes.

THE SUSPENSION
"that the room suspects"

Not confirmed. Not denied. The uncertainty is the hook. The room is held in unresolved tension.

THE HIDDEN FREQUENCY
"is a weapon"

The sacred/feral inversion. The thing beneath the surface signal. Makes the warmth retroactively dangerous.

How To Extract A Kernel

→
Ask: what does the room feel before she speaks? Not what she looks like. Not what she does. What is the pre-verbal signal — the thing that arrives before language?
→
Find the tension inside it. The strongest Kernels carry a contradiction — something that is two things at once. Sacred and feral. Soft and dangerous. Still and inevitable. The tension is the hook.
→
Test it against her worst moment. If the Kernel is still true when she has collapsed, lost everything, and is starting again from nothing — it is a real Kernel. If it only works when she is powerful — it is a status description, not a frequency.
→
Say it out loud. A Vibe Kernel that works on paper but doesn't land when spoken is not finished. It must feel like a frequency when heard — not just a sentence when read.
→
Check it against all active Kernels. Run the Collision Map on AX-04 (sacred/feral register). If two Kernels carry the same structural tension — one must be rewritten before the character is canonized.
